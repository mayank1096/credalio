GROUP BY basics

GROUP BY collects rows that share a value, so you can summarise each group with COUNT, SUM or AVG.

SELECT region, COUNT(*) AS orders
FROM sales
GROUP BY region;

Each region becomes one row, and COUNT tells you how many orders it had. Next, you’ll filter those groups with HAVING.
