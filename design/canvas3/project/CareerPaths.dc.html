How career paths work

A career path isn’t a bundle of courses. It’s an ordered route to a role, with projects and checkpoints that show what you can do.

  1. 1Choose a destinationPick the role you want to work toward, at the level you’re starting from.
  2. 2Follow the routeCourses, practice and projects in a set order, with checkpoints along the way.
  3. 3Show what you can doBuild portfolio evidence and, if you want, take the professional certification.
