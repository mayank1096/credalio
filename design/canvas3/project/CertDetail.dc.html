About this certification

This certification checks that you can prepare data, query it with SQL, find what matters and explain it to people who make decisions. You earn it through assessment against a published standard, not by finishing courses.

Who it’s for

Designed forEarly-career analysts, business professionals and people moving into data analytics.
Relevant rolesJunior Data Analyst, Reporting Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Operations Associate.
ExperienceRecommended, not required: about 6 months working with spreadsheets, reports or business data. Paid, volunteer or project work all count.

Competencies you’re assessed on

The standard you need to meet
Data preparationClean, structure and validate raw data.StandardPrepares a raw dataset for analysis with no errors that change the result.Assessed by: Case study
QueryingAccurate SQL, including joins and aggregation.StandardWrites correct SQL with joins, filters and aggregation for a business question.Assessed by: Exam + case study
AnalysisFind trends and answer business questions.StandardIdentifies the trends that matter and supports each with evidence.Assessed by: Case study
VisualisationClear charts and dashboards.StandardBuilds charts a non-specialist reads correctly the first time.Assessed by: Case study
Statistical reasoningApply basic statistics correctly.StandardUses averages, spread and comparison correctly, and says when data can’t support a claim.Assessed by: Exam
CommunicationPresent findings to people outside data.StandardExplains findings and a recommendation clearly in plain language.Assessed by: Final review
