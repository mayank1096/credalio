How professional certification works

You earn a certification by showing you meet a published standard, not by finishing courses. Each one is issued by the creator or institution named on it.

  1. 1ExploreFind a certification that matches the role or skill you want to prove.
  2. 2Check eligibilityThree questions tell you if you can register, or what to do first.
  3. 3DemonstratePrepare if you need to, then pass the exam, practical work and review.
  4. 4Get certifiedYour credential goes to your Digital Backpack with a link anyone can check.
