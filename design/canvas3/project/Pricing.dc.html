Your learning allowance

What is DLP?DLP is a learning-access unit. It isn’t cash and it isn’t a token.

Your plan gives you a monthly DLP allowance. You use it to open eligible courses, paths and certifications across Credalio.

DLPYour learning allowance. Used to access eligible learning.

How DLP works

From allowance to learning.

  1. 1SubscribeYour monthly DLP lands in your account.
  2. 2Choose learningEvery eligible course shows how much DLP it needs.
  3. 3LearnDLP is used as you open eligible learning.
  4. 4Keep goingYour allowance refreshes each month, with rollover on eligible plans.
Rollover

Life happens. Your learning can wait.On Pro, unused DLP rolls over up to 18,000. Gradual decay starts from month four. Basic follows the Basic plan policy, which is being finalised.

On Pro, the DLP you don’t use this month carries into the next one.
