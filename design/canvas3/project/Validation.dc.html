What gets reviewed

Different experts look at different things.

No single validator checks everything. Each one reviews only their own area.

Educational Validator

Educational qualityDoesn’t decide: Subject accuracy outside their expertise.

  • Clear learning outcomes
  • Lessons match the outcomes
  • Activities build understanding
  • Progression makes sense
Subject Matter Validator

Subject accuracyDoesn’t decide: Whether the learning design or assessments work.

  • Facts and technical detail
  • Relevance and depth
  • Current material
  • Practical relevance
Assessment Validator

Assessment qualityDoesn’t decide: Subject accuracy of the content.

  • Aligned with the outcomes
  • Measures real competence
  • Right difficulty
  • Fair, defensible scoring

Versioning

Validation belongs to the version reviewed.A course can’t be validated once, then rewritten under the same seal. Each validated version gets its own entry on the public record.

When a creator publishes a major update, the new version is reviewed again.
