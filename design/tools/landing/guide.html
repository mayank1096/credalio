Finding your way around

1 · The prototype (canvas)

Screens sit in five columns: landing and discovery pages; journey, onboarding and institution invite; the learner dashboard; checkout and pricing; and the pages for creators, institutions and validation. Each row is a desktop board (1440 wide) next to its mobile board (390 wide). The rows at the bottom hold the states of the discovery pages (access, eligibility, application, filters and listing states).

Press Play to click through: Explore → listing → detail → checkout, and back. Animations (the AI Coach thinking, confetti, the validation seal, the TruVault door) only run in Play mode.

2 · The developer package

index.htmlEvery screen in flow order, by section and group, with desktop and mobile links. Start here.
screens/The code: one responsive .dc.html file per screen, plus the small runtime that renders it.
tokens/Colours, type, radii, shadows, sizes, breakpoints and motion as CSS variables and JSON.
assets/ · fonts/Every image used (hero and footer art, illustrations, AI Coach characters, avatars) and Google Sans.
screens.csvEach screen's section, group, file, props, and which screens it links to.
README.mdThe detailed reference: sections, patterns, links, open items.
To open it: unzip, then run python3 -m http.server 8000 inside the folder and open localhost:8000. Opening files directly with file:// does not work (browsers block the scripts).

3 · Screen names

Titles start with a code: EXP explore, PATH career paths, CERT certifications, PROG programs, JNY journey, ONB onboarding, INST institution invite, LRN learner dashboard, ACC allowance, CHK checkout, PRC pricing, CRE for creators, ENT for institutions, TRST validation, TV TruVault. File names describe the screen and its state, for example CourseDetail-Owned or Programs-NoResults.

BUILD

Five things to know before you build

  • One file per screen, not per device. Each screen is responsive. The mobile board only previews the same file at 390 wide.
  • States are props, not copies. A board like CourseDetail-Owned just shows CourseDetail with access="owned". Build one component with a prop. screens.csv lists every board's props.
  • Values come from the code. Treat the files as a precise reference for colour, size, spacing, radius, shadow and motion, and rebuild them in your own components. The tokens folder has the shared values.
  • Build these patterns once: the site header and footer (signed-out and signed-in), the listing with filters (counts, More filters, chips, sort, compare, pagination, mobile sheet), the listing states, the detail page with its access card, the focus flow (journey, onboarding, checkout), the outcome page, the AI Coach thinking state and the info tooltip.
  • Responsive rules: the site nav collapses into a menu below 1300; breakpoints at 1080, 960 (filters become a bottom sheet, the dashboard sidebar a drawer) and 760. Touch targets are at least 44px. All motion turns off with "reduce motion".

Good to know

LinksCards, nav, breadcrumbs and buttons that lead to another screen work in the prototype. Buttons that act on the page (Save, Share, Notify me) and links to pages outside this handoff (Log in, Privacy, Terms, the course player) have no screen of their own.
DLP vs CreditsDLP is the learning allowance that comes with a plan (gold coin). Credits are the wallet, used to own learning for life and to pay certificate and program fees (cobalt "C" coin). They are separate and never converted.
Sample dataNames, prices, hours and dates are examples. Balances, counts, places left and dates are dynamic.
Open itemsFinal prices and fees are for the client to confirm; the learner dashboard's sample prices are not yet aligned with the listing pages. The lesson preview covers video and reading lessons.
FontGoogle Sans, from Google Fonts. Please confirm its licence before shipping; Google Sans Flex or Inter are the closest alternatives.
