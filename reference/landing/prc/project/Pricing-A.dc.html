Your learning allowance

What is DLP?

DLP is Credalio’s learning-access unit. Your subscription gives you a monthly DLP allowance that you use to access eligible learning experiences across Credalio.

DLP is a platform learning-access unit. It is not cash and not a token.
Free isn’t a trial that ends. Your 200 DLP limits DLP-bearing learning, not your access to Credalio: you can still browse, discover with your AI Coach, keep your profile and Backpack, and use eligible free resources.
