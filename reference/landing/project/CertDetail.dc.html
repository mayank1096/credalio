Overview

This certification confirms that you can work through the full analysis cycle: preparing data, querying it, finding patterns, and communicating what they mean. It’s assessed, not just completed, so employers can trust what it says about you.

Who it’s for

Career changers moving into analyst rolesEarly-career analysts who want recognised proofProfessionals who work with data and want to formalise their skills

Competencies

Data preparationClean, structure and validate raw data.
QueryingWrite accurate SQL, including joins and aggregation.
AnalysisFind trends and answer business questions.
VisualisationBuild clear charts and dashboards.
Statistical reasoningApply basic statistics correctly.
CommunicationPresent findings to non-technical audiences.
