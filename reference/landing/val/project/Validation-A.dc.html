Credalio Validation

See what stands behind the learning.

Credalio makes the validation status of learning experiences visible. Where an experience has been submitted for validation, qualified validators independently review the areas relevant to their expertise before a validation decision is made.

What gets reviewed

Different experts look at different things.

No single validator checks everything. Each reviews only the areas within their expertise.

Educational Quality

Educational Validator

Reviews whether:
  • learning outcomes are clear
  • lessons align with those outcomes
  • learning activities support understanding
  • progression makes educational sense
  • the experience is appropriately structured
What they don’t determineSubject-matter correctness outside their expertise.
Subject Accuracy

Subject Matter Validator

Reviews:
  • factual accuracy
  • technical correctness
  • relevance to the stated field
  • appropriate depth
  • currency of material where applicable
  • practical relevance
What they don’t determineWhether the learning design or assessments are effective.
Assessment Quality

Assessment Validator

Reviews whether:
  • assessments align with learning outcomes
  • questions appropriately measure competence
  • difficulty is appropriate
  • scoring is defensible
  • practical assessments test what they claim to test
What they don’t determineSubject accuracy of the content being taught.
AI QualityConditional

AI Validator

Appears where the learning experience uses relevant AI functionality. Reviews AI-related behaviour, implementation and appropriate controls within the applicable validation scope.

Security & PrivacyConditional

Security & Privacy Validator

Used where the learning experience requires this specialist review, such as handling personal data or connected systems.

The validation team is matched to what is actually being validated.
